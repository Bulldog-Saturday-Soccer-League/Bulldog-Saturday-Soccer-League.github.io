League Info

Information

When: Every Saturday 9:00 to 10:00 AM Starting December 5th
Where: Fleet Fields
Who: All Intermediate 4th and 5th graders
How: We will have a few teams and will be playing games against each other. The league is free of charge and is open to all players regardless of skill level.
Why: We believe that Bulldogs should have the opportunity to play soccer, and have an opportunity to have fun playing soccer with school friends.
